# Dynamic 100-altcoin universe

BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT are mandatory CORE and never consume altcoin slots. Default altcoin_count=100; smaller explicit configuration remains available for tests/local deployments. Persist each selection with selected_at, first_seen, canonical ID, spot/OKX metadata, score components, tier, pinned status, raw lineage and quality gaps. Membership changes are append-only for historical replay.

Filter TRADING spot USDT pairs, stable/wrapped-stable and leveraged assets, non-positive volume/price and unusable metadata. Prefer exact live linear USDT OKX SWAP matches; no synthetic prefix mapping. Explicit pinned spot pairs remain within the 100 slots and cannot vanish solely from falling rank. Missing/non-trading pinned pairs are reported, never substituted silently. Delisting prediction is NEEDS_VERIFICATION; known non-live metadata is excluded; future delisting/expiry monitoring is not implemented.

Daily scoring uses configurable liquidity/spot turnover/derivative activity/volatility/importance/data-quality weights (30/25/20/10/10/5). Unavailable 30-day history, market cap and full depth are marked missing rather than fabricated; score coverage is displayed. Observed spread/spot turnover/OKX base-volume × last-price notional are limited proxies with explicit definitions, not claimed 30-day liquidity or market cap. Daily replacement cap=10; forced invalid removals are separately recorded. Tier1=20, Tier2=30, remaining Tier3. Core/Tier1 get details most frequently; remaining detail slots rotate fairly, with persistent checkpoint. All selected prices and bulk OI refresh every cycle. OI history provides real 5-minute reference points independently of detail rotation.
